# UI / Marketing Gap: DriveGo vs daotaolaixelangdaihoc.vn

Date: 2026-06-29

## Scope

Compare public-facing DriveGo UI and marketing flow with `https://daotaolaixelangdaihoc.vn/`.

This file intentionally does not inspect or change the question-bank database. See `docs/QUESTION-BANK-LOCK.md`.

## What The Reference Site Emphasizes

The reference site is a traditional driving-center marketing site. Its public page focuses on:

- Clear center identity: `Trung Tâm Dạy Nghề Đào Tạo & Sát Hạch Lái Xe Làng Đại Học`.
- Immediate registration form on the landing page.
- Hotline and email shown near the top.
- Simple navigation: home, about, online registration, video, lookup, contact.
- Service selector for A1 and A motorcycle license registration.
- Trust copy: many years of experience, professional environment, modern equipment, experienced instructors.
- Practical conversion details: public fees, simple paperwork, fast registration support, theory study tips, online classes, stable class time.
- SEO metadata targeting keywords such as `học lái xe`, `A1`, `A2`, `B1`, `B2`, `học phí trọn gói`, `cam kết không phát sinh`, and `tỉ lệ đỗ cao`.
- Blog/news content around age, health requirements, 2025 exam changes, fake license risks, and related traffic-law topics.

## What DriveGo Already Has

- Modern app-like UI instead of a static marketing site.
- Auth, student/admin routing, profile, enrollment, schedules, documents, payments, notifications, theory, exams, and history.
- Day/night theme support with a visible toggle.
- Online learning/exam workflow that goes beyond a marketing lead form.

## Gaps To Consider

1. **Landing-page conversion form**
   DriveGo should expose a lightweight public lead form on the first page: name, phone, license class, preferred center/time, submit.

2. **Hotline and Zalo-style quick contact**
   Add persistent contact actions on mobile and desktop. The reference site makes hotline/email immediately visible.

3. **About / trust section**
   Add a concrete section for training-center credibility: instructors, practice yards, support process, exam support, and success claims if verifiable.

4. **Fee and paperwork transparency**
   Add a public block for expected fees, included services, and required documents by license class.

5. **Class schedule preview**
   DriveGo has schedule features after login, but the public page should show a simple preview: upcoming sessions, online/offline options, and flexible times.

6. **License-class landing pages**
   Create SEO-friendly pages for A1, A2, B1, and B2 instead of relying only on the app flow.

7. **SEO metadata**
   Current public metadata is thin. Add per-page title/description/OG content for keywords around driving lessons, license classes, exam prep, and online registration.

8. **Content/blog layer**
   Add short public articles for health requirements, age requirements, 2025 exam changes, hồ sơ cần chuẩn bị, lệ phí, and exam tips.

9. **Video/social proof**
   The reference site has a `VIDEO` navigation item. DriveGo could add practice-yard clips, simulator clips, or short guide videos.

10. **Contact page completeness**
    Add address, map, hotline, email, opening hours, and a callback form.

## Suggested Priority

1. Public lead form + hotline/contact actions.
2. SEO metadata and license-class landing pages.
3. Fees/documents/schedule preview.
4. About/trust/social proof.
5. Blog/video content.
